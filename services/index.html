Quick answer

What trucking services does Texas Solutions offer besides dispatch?

Besides dispatch, Texas Solutions helps carriers get started (MC authority setup, MC lease, compliance and permits, factoring setup), stay compliant (IFTA and 2290 filing, safety audit prep, ELD and logs, DOT drug testing) and grow (bookkeeping, collections, driver hiring, carrier websites, SEO and social media). Government fees, insurance and taxes are paid by the carrier.
