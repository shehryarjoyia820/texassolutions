Quick answer

How is the Texas Solutions dispatch fee calculated?

Multiply your truck's weekly gross by the dispatch percentage: 5-6% for OTR semi trucks, 8-10% for OTR small trucks. Example: a semi grossing $9,000 a week pays about $450-$540 a week. A hotshot grossing $8,000 pays about $640-$800. There is no flat rate and no setup fee.
