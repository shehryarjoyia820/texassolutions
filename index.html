Common questions

BEFORE YOU GET STARTED

Does Texas Solutions provide the dispatch service directly?+

Yes. Texas Solutions provides truck dispatch services directly to carriers that sign a Texas Solutions dispatch agreement. Your application is not sold as a lead to outside lead buyers.

How is the dispatch fee charged?+

Dispatch service is percentage-based according to your signed agreement. There is no setup fee and no monthly subscription.

Are there upfront costs?+

No Texas Solutions dispatch fee is charged upfront. Applicable service terms are explained before you enter a paid dispatch relationship.

Do you guarantee rates or earnings?+

No. Freight volume, rates, operating costs, market conditions, and carrier performance vary. Texas Solutions does not guarantee any specific load volume, rate, revenue, or earnings.

Will I be forced to accept loads?+

No. You remain in control of your business and decide whether a load, lane, and rate work for you.
