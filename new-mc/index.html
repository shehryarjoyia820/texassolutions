Quick answer

How do I start with a new MC number?

Set up your business and EIN, apply to FMCSA for a USDOT number and MC authority, have your insurance and BOC-3 filed, wait out the protest period, register UCR, IRP, IFTA and drug testing, then send a complete carrier packet to brokers that accept new authority and book your first load.

Step by step

From USDOT number to first load

  1. 01

    Set up the business

    Form your LLC or corporation, get an EIN and open a business bank account, so the authority is in the right legal name.

    How to start a trucking company →

  2. 02

    Apply for USDOT and MC authority

    Register with FMCSA for a USDOT number and for-hire operating authority (your MC number). Check every answer before you submit; mistakes cost weeks.

    MC number and authority setup → How to get your MC number →

  3. 03

    File insurance and BOC-3

    Your insurance agent files proof of liability coverage with FMCSA, and a process agent files your BOC-3. Authority cannot go active without both.

    Partner referrals for insurance →

  4. 04

    Wait out the protest period

    FMCSA posts your application for a protest period. Use the time to prepare your broker packet, choose a factoring company and set up your ELD.

    Freight factoring explained →

  5. 05

    Register UCR, IRP, IFTA and drug testing

    Interstate carriers register for UCR, IRP plates and an IFTA account in their base state, and join a drug and alcohol consortium before the first load.

    IFTA for owner-operators → Compliance and permits →

  6. 06

    Get set up with brokers and book the first load

    Send one complete carrier packet to brokers that accept new authority, then book loads that leave you in a good market for the next one.

    How to get loads with a new MC → Dispatch for new authority →

Checklist before your first load

  • Active MC authority and USDOT number
  • Certificate of insurance listing auto liability and cargo
  • W-9 in the legal business name
  • Notice of assignment, if you use factoring
  • Copy of your CDL (owner-operator)
  • Voided check or ACH details
  • ELD installed and working
  • Drug and alcohol consortium enrollment
  • Signed dispatch agreement

FAQ

New MC questions

How long does it take to get MC authority?

Many carriers see their authority go active a few weeks after applying, once insurance and BOC-3 filings are on record and the protest period has passed. Timelines vary, so check your status on FMCSA.

Can I get loads with a brand-new MC number?

Yes. Some brokers book new authorities from day one if your packet is complete and insurance is verified. A dispatcher who works with new MCs knows which ones.

What if I do not have my own authority yet?

You can lease on to an established partner carrier's authority with our MC lease program and haul while your own authority is processed.

Do you help with the filings?

Yes. MC authority setup is $275 one-time. Government fees, insurance and BOC-3 costs are paid directly by you.
