1. Who we are

Texas Solutions provides truck dispatch services to motor carriers. Texas Solutions is not a motor carrier and is not a freight broker. These terms govern your use of this website; paid dispatch service is governed by the signed Texas Solutions dispatch agreement, which controls if the two ever conflict.

2. The service

We search available freight, review lanes and deadhead, communicate and negotiate with brokers, and help keep rate confirmations and dispatch information organized. You approve every load, lane and rate. We do not book freight you have not authorized.

3. Carrier responsibilities

You are responsible for maintaining active operating authority and insurance, for compliance with all applicable federal and state regulations, for the safe and lawful operation of your equipment, and for the accuracy of the information and documents you provide to us.

4. Fees

Dispatch service is percentage-based under the terms agreed to in your dispatch agreement. There is no setup fee and no monthly subscription. Pricing is discussed directly with each carrier before paid service begins.
