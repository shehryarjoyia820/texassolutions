Box trucks are one of the easiest ways into owner-operator trucking, but the freight works differently from semi loads: smaller, more frequent, and often with tighter delivery windows. Here is how to keep a box truck loaded and what it costs to run.

## Where box truck loads come from

- **Load boards.** The biggest source of spot freight, and the most competitive.
- **Direct broker relationships.** Brokers who know your truck, liftgate and service will call you first.
- **Dispatch services.** A dispatcher searches the boards, calls brokers and negotiates for you, so your time goes into driving.

Match loads to your box length, liftgate and payload before you book, and ask about lumper, appointment and detention terms.

## Box truck fuel cost

A 26 ft diesel box truck typically gets about **11 mpg empty and 8.5 mpg fully loaded**. At $6.00 a gallon that is roughly **$0.55-$0.70 per mile** in diesel. Box truck rates per mile run about $1.80-$3.20 on our [rate guide](/truck-dispatch-rates.html), so fuel is a big share of every load.

Check your own numbers with the [box truck fuel calculator](/truck-fuel-cost-calculator.html?truck=box-truck#fuelCalc).

## What box truck dispatch costs

Texas Solutions charges **10% of weekly gross** for OTR box trucks and straight trucks. On typical weekly gross of $7,000-$9,000 that is about **$700-$900 a week**, with no flat rate, no setup fee and no monthly subscription.

Box truck dispatch costs a higher percentage than semi dispatch (5%) because each dollar of box truck gross takes more loads, more broker setups and more paperwork.
