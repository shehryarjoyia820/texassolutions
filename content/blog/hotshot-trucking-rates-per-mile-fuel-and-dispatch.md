Hotshot trucking moves time-sensitive and partial loads with a heavy-duty pickup and a flatbed or gooseneck trailer. It is fast, flexible, and popular in Texas and the Permian Basin, where equipment and oilfield freight cannot wait for a full truckload.

## Hotshot rates per mile

As a rough guide, hotshot loads often pay about **$4 to $5 a mile**. What moves the number:

- **Urgency.** Same-day and next-morning freight pays more.
- **Lane.** Loads into areas with little return freight need a higher rate to cover the trip home.
- **Season and region.** Energy and construction activity swing demand.

Compare that with other equipment on our [rate-per-mile guide](/truck-dispatch-rates.html). Rates are market-driven and never guaranteed.

## Hotshot fuel mileage by load weight

Weight matters more on a hotshot than on a semi, because the cargo is a large share of the total weight. Typical figures for a 1-ton diesel dually and 40 ft gooseneck:

| Load on trailer | Typical mpg |
|---|---|
| Empty | about 13-14 mpg |
| About 9,000 lbs | about 10 mpg |
| About 16,000 lbs | about 7-9 mpg |

At $6.00 a gallon, a loaded hotshot averaging 9 mpg spends about **$0.67 per mile** on diesel. Enter your own weight, miles and fuel state in the [hotshot fuel calculator](/truck-fuel-cost-calculator.html?truck=hotshot#fuelCalc).

## What a hotshot dispatcher does

- Finds expedited and partial loads that fit your trailer, weight rating and permits
- Negotiates the rate and confirms dimensions and securement needs
- Plans the next load so you are not driving home empty
- Handles broker setups, packets and rate confirmations

You approve every load before it is booked.
