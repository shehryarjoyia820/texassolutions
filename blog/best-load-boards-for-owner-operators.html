Best Load Boards for Owner-Operators and How to Use Them

By Texas Solutions Dispatch Team · September 23, 2026 · 2 min read

Load boards Getting started
Quick answer

What are the best load boards for owner-operators?

Popular load boards include DAT One, Truckstop and 123Loadboard, plus specialty boards for hotshot, flatbed and power only. The best one depends on your equipment and lanes: look for fresh postings, rate data, broker credit information, and easy filtering. Many carriers use two boards and vet every broker before booking.

In this article
  1. Common load boards
  2. What to look for
  3. How to vet a broker before you book
  4. Find loads that actually pay
  5. When a dispatcher makes sense

A load board is a marketplace where brokers and shippers post freight and carriers search for it. For most owner-operators it is the main source of spot loads, so choosing and using one well matters.

Common load boards

Board Known for
DAT One One of the largest general boards; rate data and market tools
Truckstop Large general board with broker credit and payment tools
123Loadboard Lower-cost option, popular with newer carriers and small fleets
Direct Freight and others Additional volume; specialty and regional boards

Features and pricing change often, so use free trials where offered and test with your own lanes before committing.

What to look for

  • Fresh postings. Old loads waste time. Look for posted-time stamps and real-time updates.
  • Filters that fit your equipment: dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck, power only, hotshot.
  • Rate data. Market averages help you tell a good rate from a bad one.
  • Broker credit and days-to-pay. Getting paid late costs as much as a low rate.
  • Mobile app and load alerts for your lanes.
  • Integrations with your ELD, TMS or factoring company.

How to vet a broker before you book

Freight fraud, including fake brokers and double brokering, costs carriers real money. Before you accept:

  1. Confirm active broker authority and bond on FMCSA's website.
  2. Check the company's payment history and reviews.
  3. Make sure the phone number and email match their official record.
  4. Get a signed rate confirmation with the agreed rate, pickup and delivery details, and payment terms.
  5. Be wary of rates far above market, pressure to rush, or requests to change payment details.

Find loads that actually pay

Do not chase the highest rate per mile alone. Compare each load on its rate per total mile, including deadhead and against your cost per mile. Use the load profitability calculator and the deadhead miles calculator, and know your number with the cost per mile calculator.

When a dispatcher makes sense

Searching boards, calling brokers and negotiating takes hours every day. A dispatcher does that for a percentage of your weekly gross: at Texas Solutions it is 5% for semis, 8% for hotshots and 10% for box trucks, OTR, with no flat rate. You approve every load. Get a free estimate.

Frequently asked questions

Which load board has the most loads?

DAT and Truckstop are the two largest general truckload boards in the US. Exact volumes change constantly, so trial each for your equipment and lanes before paying for a subscription.

How do I check if a freight broker is legitimate?

Verify the broker's active authority and bond on the FMCSA website, check their payment history and credit rating, confirm the contact details match their official record, and get a signed rate confirmation before pickup.

Can a dispatcher use load boards for me?

Yes. Dispatch services search load boards and broker networks on your behalf, negotiate rates, and book under your authority, so you spend less time online and more time driving.
