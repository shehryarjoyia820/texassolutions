Free Trucking Calculators

15 free calculators: dispatch fees, fuel cost and surcharge, detention, cost per mile, load profit, IFTA mileage, HOS hours and more. No signup.

Your numbers stay with you. Calculations run in your browser and nothing you type is sent to us. If you tap Save on this device, your inputs are kept only in this browser's local storage on this device, never on our servers, and you can clear them with Reset. We use Google Analytics to count tool use (which tool, not what you typed). Forms are different: when you send an inquiry, the details you enter are emailed to our dispatch team.
