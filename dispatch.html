Percentage vs flat fee

Which pricing fits your truck?

Both models are fair; they suit different trucks. We only charge a percentage, so a week the truck sits costs you nothing.

Percentage of grossFlat weekly fee
How it is chargedA percentage of what the truck grosses that weekThe same dollar amount every week
Slow week or truck in the shopYou pay little or nothingThe full fee is still due
Big weekThe fee grows with your grossThe fee stays the same
Best forNew authorities, part-time, seasonal and home-time-heavy trucksEstablished trucks with steady high gross
What the dispatcher is paid forHigher rates and more loaded milesKeeping you signed up

Quick answer

Which trucks does Texas Solutions dispatch?

Texas Solutions dispatches semi trucks pulling dry van, reefer, flatbed, step deck and power only, plus hotshots, box trucks and straight trucks, for owner-operators, small fleets and carriers with new authority across the USA. Owner-operators without their own authority can use the MC lease program.

Included

What dispatch covers

  • Load search and rate negotiation
  • Lane and deadhead planning
  • Broker credit and authority checks before you book
  • Carrier setup packets and rate confirmations
  • Invoices to you or your factoring company
  • Detention, layover and TONU follow-up
  • A weekly gross and load summary
  • No long-term contract, no forced dispatch
